This app smoothly animates your GPX track on the map with customizable speed, zoom, map style and ending scenario — then lets you record it as a ready-made video clip you can drop straight into your edit.

Upload a GPX file from your run, ride or hike using the "Load GPX" button in the sidebar. Your track will appear on the map, ready to animate or record as a video.

info Rendering happens entirely in your browser. For the smoothest animation, close other tabs/apps, plug in your laptop and avoid battery-saver mode so it can run at full performance.
